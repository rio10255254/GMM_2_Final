usable Lab reconstruction model because it still moves real checkboard corner
responses away from the raw corner locations.

## Guard Interpretation

The validation metrics improved strongly from the starting point to the end of
training, but the later epochs repeatedly triggered boundary-related guards.

| Signal | Count |
|---|---:|
| Thickening guard flags in CSV | 29 epochs |
| Oversmooth guard flags in CSV | 89 epochs |
| Lap/boundary correction guard flags in CSV | 188 epochs |
| PSF guard flags in CSV | 0 epochs |
| Stdout band guard lines | 82 |
| Stdout thickening guard lines | 43 |
| Stdout lap guard lines | 105 |

After epoch 60, the trainer intentionally stopped replacing the official best
checkpoint. There were 149 later "skip update" messages because the configured
best checkpoint window was epoch 10 through epoch 60.

Plain interpretation: the final model became cleaner by ordinary validation
metrics, but it also moved into a boundary-risk region. That is why the epoch
33 checkpoint remains the safer official checkpoint for the next real-sequence
test.
