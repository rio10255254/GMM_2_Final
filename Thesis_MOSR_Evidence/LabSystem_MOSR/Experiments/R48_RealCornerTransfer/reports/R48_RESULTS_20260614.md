# R48 Results - 2026-06-14

## Verdict

R48 completed its formal 200-epoch Lab R39-start training run, but it did not
solve the real Lab checkboard rounded-corner problem.

The training loss and held-out validation numbers improved through epoch 200,
but real checkboard reconstruction showed stronger corner artifacts than the
earlier Lab controls. R48 is therefore a negative Lab-system transfer result and
must not be promoted.

## Interpretation

R48 improved its own training/held-out validation distribution, but it made the
actual Lab checkboard corners worse. The contact sheet shows stronger white/black
corner artifacts and local edge distortion around the real checkboard corners.

This means the new synthetic real-corner package still does not match the real
failure mode closely enough. The next redesign should not simply add more of
the same R48-style data; it needs a tighter real-image anchored target or a
more constrained reconstruction objective that prevents haloed corner shifts.
