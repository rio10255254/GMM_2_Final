## Interpretation

R49 did not fail because training crashed. It failed because the model learned a
change that does not preserve the real checker corners. The best-tradeoff and
detail-guarded outputs are too conservative and move the strongest corner
response far from the raw corner. The latest output is stronger, but it creates
more widespread >5 px corner shifts and visible edge artifacts.

The CAD-anchor idea should not be continued by simply increasing epochs or
anchor ratio. The next design needs a direct real-sequence constraint on corner
response position.
