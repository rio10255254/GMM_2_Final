[18] heading 1 摘要

[19]  隨著半導體與精密製造的特徵尺寸持續縮小，顯微影像重建若要支援量測，不能只以影像銳利度判斷，也必須確認輪廓與相鄰影格位置是否穩定。本研究提出量測導向超解析（Measurement-Oriented Super-Resolution, MOSR），以點擴散函數先驗、抗混疊上取樣及以輸入影像為基準的殘差輸出，限制重建對原始幾何的非必要改動。

[20]  所有方法只採一個代表性輪廓點：先由Chan–Vese主動輪廓模型取得完整粗輪廓，再沿局部法線取正確極性的最大灰階梯度位置作為亞像素量測點。LSC使用全部輪廓點提供位移中心，MZC評定最小區域圓度，MIC與MCC提供尺寸包絡；另以相同120組配對直接驗證LSC與MZC的中心適用性。

[21]  在36顆pillar、12種曝光的SEM配對資料中，MOSR使徑向形狀誤差由48.4降至42.8 nm（降低11.6%），輪廓相關係數由0.557提高至0.877（提高57.5%），MZC圓度誤差由110.6降至26.5 nm（降低76.0%）。四組序列共120組鎖定配對中，MOSR相對Raw使逐步位移標準差、名義步距誤差及陣列內離散度分別降低17.9%、18.0%與61.1%；相對Wiener則降低7.8%、10.5%與18.4%。MZC圓心在Raw、Wiener與MOSR的三項位移指標均顯著高於LSC，確認LSC適合作為位移中心、MZC保留為圓度評定。

COMMENTS 3

[22]  八模型比較中，MOSR的名義步距誤差為5.74 nm、排名第1，逐步位移標準差為7.29 nm、排名第3。統一FP32效率測試顯示，MOSR處理2048×1536影像的中位時間為1.10 s、速度2.85 MP/s，在八模型中排名第4，且比同參數量級EDSR快5.23倍。MOSR因而呈現輪廓、位移與運算效率之間的量測導向平衡，而非僅改善視覺銳利度。

COMMENTS 5

[23]  關鍵字：量測導向超解析、主動輪廓、最大梯度位置、最小平方圓、最小區域圓、微位移量測。

Abstract

[25]  Microscopy reconstruction intended for metrology must be assessed by contour and inter-frame positional stability rather than visual sharpness alone. This thesis proposes Measurement-Oriented Super-Resolution (MOSR), combining point-spread-function priors, anti-aliased upsampling, and an input-referenced residual output to limit unnecessary changes to the measured geometry.

[26]  All methods use one representative contour point. A Chan–Vese active contour supplies a complete coarse contour, after which the correct-polarity maximum-gradient location along each local normal defines the subpixel point. The LSC uses all contour points to provide the displacement center; MZC evaluates minimum-zone roundness, and MIC/MCC provide size envelopes. LSC and MZC center suitability is tested directly on the same 120 pairs.

[27]  In the 36-pillar, 12-exposure SEM-paired data, MOSR reduced radial-shape error from 48.4 to 42.8 nm (11.6%), increased contour correlation from 0.557 to 0.877 (57.5%), and reduced MZC roundness error from 110.6 to 26.5 nm (76.0%). Across 120 locked displacement pairs, MOSR reduced step standard deviation, nominal-step error, and within-grid dispersion by 17.9%, 18.0%, and 61.1% relative to Raw, and by 7.8%, 10.5%, and 18.4% relative to Wiener. MZC centers produced significantly higher values than LSC centers for all three displacement metrics in Raw, Wiener, and MOSR, supporting LSC for displacement and MZC for roundness.

COMMENTS 9

[28]  In the eight-model comparison, MOSR ranked first in nominal-step error (5.74 nm) and third in step displacement standard deviation (7.29 nm). Under a uniform FP32 efficiency protocol, its median time for a 2048×1536 image was 1.10 s (2.85 MP/s), ranking fourth of eight and running 5.23 times faster than the similarly sized EDSR. MOSR therefore provides a measurement-oriented balance among contour fidelity, displacement stability, and computational efficiency rather than visual sharpening alone.

COMMENTS 11

[29]  Keywords: Measurement-Oriented Super-Resolution; Active Contour; Maximum-Gradient Location; Least-Squares Circle; Minimum-Zone Circle; Micro-Displacement Metrology.
